# Physiotherapist Hiring — Paste-Ready Recruitment Copy

Posts for LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Instagram. Every link points at a live page on the site.

- Careers hub: https://www.physioatyourdoorstep.com/careers
- Area page pattern: https://www.physioatyourdoorstep.com/careers/physiotherapist-jobs-in-<area>

## 1. LinkedIn — main hiring post (all areas)

> **We're hiring physiotherapists across Bangalore.**
>
> Physio At Your Doorstep has grown from a small idea in 2021 into a network of 300+ physiotherapists delivering rehabilitation at home across Bangalore and Pune. We're now expanding our Bangalore clinical team across 24 areas — from Koramangala and HSR Layout to Whitefield, Indiranagar, Electronic City and Jayanagar.
>
> **The role:** home-visit physiotherapy. You assess the patient, build the plan, deliver the sessions and progress them — with senior physiotherapists and structured protocols behind you. Patients come to us; you focus on the rehabilitation.
>
> **Three ways to work:**
> • Full-time — a fixed patient load in one area
> • Part-time — morning or evening slots around your clinic or MPT
> • Freelance — per visit, in the areas you already travel through
>
> **You'll need:** BPT minimum (MPT preferred), 1–2 years of hands-on clinical experience, and your own two-wheeler.
>
> **What we offer:** patients brought to you, work close to home, clinical mentoring, and a case mix that spans sports, post-operative, orthopaedic, neurological, geriatric and paediatric rehab.
>
> Full details and the application form (attach your CV directly):
> https://www.physioatyourdoorstep.com/careers
>
> Know a good physiotherapist in Bangalore? Please tag or share.
>
