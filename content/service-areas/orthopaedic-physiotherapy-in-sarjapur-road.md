## Orthopaedic physiotherapy — and who it helps

Orthopaedic physiotherapy treats problems of the musculoskeletal system: the back, neck, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. Along Sarjapur Road, that most often means desk-driven lower-back and neck pain, knee stiffness, frozen shoulder, sciatica and disc-related pain — the everyday cost of long working hours and even longer commutes.

Every case begins with a thorough hands-on assessment. Your physiotherapist checks posture, joint range, strength and movement, works out what is actually driving the pain, and builds a personalised plan to reduce it, restore movement and stop it returning. The aim is lasting relief by fixing the cause — not a short-lived patch on the symptom.
