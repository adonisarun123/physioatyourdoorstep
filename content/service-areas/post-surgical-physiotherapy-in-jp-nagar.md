## Post-surgical rehabilitation — and why it matters

The surgery is only half the journey. Structured physiotherapy afterwards restores movement, rebuilds strength, reduces stiffness and helps you return to daily life — and it lowers the risk of ending up with a stiff, weak joint that never fully recovers.

We rehabilitate patients after knee and hip replacements, ligament repairs, spine surgery, rotator cuff repairs and fracture fixation, always working within the timeline and precautions your surgeon has set.

## Post-surgery physiotherapy in JP Nagar: why home care is safer here

In the first weeks after surgery, travel is exactly what you want to avoid. Getting into a car, sitting out JP Nagar and Bannerghatta Road traffic, and navigating a clinic on crutches or a walker is uncomfortable, tiring and carries real risk. Home rehabilitation removes all of it: your physiotherapist comes to you and works with your actual stairs, bed, sofa and bathroom, helping you move safely around the space you live in.

It also protects consistency in those critical early weeks, when doing the right movements at the right time — and avoiding the wrong ones — makes the biggest difference to your final result. Research on knee-replacement recovery has found well-structured home-based rehab can be comparable to outpatient rehab for restoring knee function.

## Our treatment approach

Rehabilitation is staged and protocol-led. Early sessions protect the surgical site while restoring gentle, controlled movement and managing swelling; the middle phase rebuilds strength, balance and endurance; the final phase restores the functional movements you need for daily life and, where relevant, work or sport.

- Protected range-of-motion work in the early phase
- Swelling and pain management
- Progressive strengthening as healing allows
- Gait, balance and stair-safety training
- Scar and soft-tissue mobilisation where appropriate
- Close alignment with your surgeon's protocol and precautions

Often within the first days, guided by your surgeon's protocol. Early, appropriate movement helps restore range and reduce stiffness — we always work within your surgeon's timeline and precautions.
