## Pediatric physiotherapy — helping children move and grow

Pediatric physiotherapy supports babies and children with movement and developmental challenges — helping them build strength, coordination, balance and the motor skills they need to reach their milestones. It can begin very early — even in infancy — when a delay or condition is identified. Early, appropriate support is valuable because young children's development is highly responsive.

For many children, yes — a familiar, comfortable setting helps them engage, and it's easier for parents. We'll advise if any equipment-based clinic input would help.
