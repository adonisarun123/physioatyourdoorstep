## Pediatric physiotherapy — helping children move and grow

Pediatric physiotherapy supports babies and children with movement and developmental challenges — helping them build the strength, coordination, balance and motor skills they need to reach their milestones. Sessions are play-based, so therapy feels like fun rather than a chore, and parents are involved every step of the way.

Early support matters. A young child's brain is highly adaptable, and appropriate, consistent physiotherapy during these years helps build movement skills and supports development.
