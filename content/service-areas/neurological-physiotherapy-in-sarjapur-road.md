## Neurological physiotherapy — restoring movement and independence

Neurological physiotherapy helps people affected by conditions of the brain, spinal cord and nerves regain as much movement, balance and independence as possible. Recovery works by encouraging the nervous system to relearn movement through repeated, task-specific practice — which is exactly why consistency and the right environment matter so much.

We work with stroke and paralysis, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, neuropathy, Bell's palsy and balance and gait disorders, always alongside the patient's neurologist and medical team.

Importantly, neurological rehabilitation is about far more than walking again. It also restores the everyday functions that give a person their independence — dressing, using the bathroom, moving safely from bed to chair — and helps rebuild the confidence that a stroke or a diagnosis so often takes away.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should stroke rehabilitation start?

Rehabilitation generally begins as early as the medical team advises — earlier, consistent input tends to support better recovery. We coordinate with the treating neurologist and work to their guidance.

### Can you provide Parkinson's physiotherapy at home?

Yes — home programmes for Parkinson's focus on movement, balance, posture and gait to help maintain mobility and independence, alongside the neurologist's care.

### Do you train family members and caregivers?

Yes. Because neuro rehabilitation depends on consistency, we show caregivers how to support safe movement and exercises between our sessions.

### How long will recovery take?

Neurological recovery is gradual and varies a great deal from person to person. Your physiotherapist sets realistic goals and reviews progress regularly rather than promising fixed timelines.

### Do you help with bed-to-chair transfers and walking again?

Yes — safe transfers, standing, walking and stair practice are central to home neuro rehab, and being in your own home makes this training directly relevant.
