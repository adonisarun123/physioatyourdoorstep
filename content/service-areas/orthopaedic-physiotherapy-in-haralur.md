## Orthopaedic physiotherapy — and who it helps

Orthopaedic physiotherapy treats problems of the musculoskeletal system: the back, neck, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. Along the Haralur corridor it most often means desk-driven lower-back and neck pain, knee stiffness, frozen shoulder, sciatica and disc-related pain — the everyday toll of long IT working hours and long commutes.

Every case starts with a thorough hands-on assessment. ## Our treatment approach

There is no generic routine here. After the assessment, your physiotherapist combines exactly the techniques your condition needs and progresses them as you improve.

- Pain and inflammation control
- Joint mobilisation to restore movement
- Progressive strengthening and endurance work
- Stretching, flexibility and posture correction
- Ergonomic guidance for your desk and home workstation
- Balance, coordination and gait retraining where needed

The programme advances gradually, guided by your pain levels, healing stage and goals — so you build genuine, lasting strength rather than chasing quick, temporary relief.
