Orthopaedic physiotherapy treats problems of the musculoskeletal system: the back, neck, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. ## Our treatment approach

There is no generic routine here. After the assessment, your physiotherapist combines exactly the techniques your condition needs and progresses them as you improve.

- Pain and inflammation control
- Joint mobilisation to restore movement
- Progressive strengthening and endurance work
- Stretching, flexibility and posture correction
- Ergonomic guidance for your desk and home setup
- Balance, coordination and gait retraining where needed

The programme is progressed gradually, guided by your pain, healing stage and goals — so you build lasting strength rather than chasing quick, temporary relief.
