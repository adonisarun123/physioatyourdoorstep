## Post-surgical rehabilitation — and why it matters

The surgery is only half the recovery. Structured physiotherapy afterwards is what restores movement, rebuilds strength, reduces stiffness and helps you return to daily life — and it lowers the risk of a stiff, weak joint that never quite recovers.

We rehab patients after knee and hip replacements, ACL reconstruction, spine surgery, rotator cuff repair and fracture fixation, always working within the timeline and precautions your surgeon has set. You can read more about our full [post-surgical physiotherapy service](/service/post-surgical-physiotherapy) and how it is delivered across Bangalore.

## Why home care is safer in HSR Layout

In the first weeks after surgery, travel is exactly what you want to avoid — climbing into a car, sitting through HSR and Silk Board traffic, and navigating a clinic on crutches is risky and exhausting. Home rehabilitation removes all of that: your physiotherapist comes to you, works with your actual stairs, bed and bathroom, and helps you move safely around your own home.

It also means consistency in those critical early weeks, when doing the right movements at the right time makes the biggest difference to the final result.

## Our treatment approach

Rehab is staged and protocol-led. Early sessions protect the surgical site while restoring gentle, controlled movement and managing swelling; later stages build strength, balance and endurance; the final phase restores the functional movements you need for daily life and, where relevant, work or sport.

- Protected range-of-motion work in the early phase
- Swelling and pain management
- Progressive strengthening as healing allows
- Gait, balance and stair-safety training
- Scar and soft-tissue mobilisation where appropriate
- Close alignment with your surgeon's protocol and precautions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should physiotherapy start after surgery?

Often within the first days, guided by your surgeon's protocol. Early, appropriate movement helps restore range and reduce stiffness — we always work within your surgeon's timeline and precautions.

### Is home rehab safe right after surgery?

Yes — and it avoids risky post-op travel. We work with your real home environment, help you move safely, and progress only as healing allows.
