## Neurological physiotherapy — restoring movement and independence

Neurological physiotherapy helps people affected by conditions of the brain, spinal cord and nerves regain as much movement, balance and independence as possible. Recovery works by encouraging the nervous system to relearn movement through repeated, task-specific practice — which is why consistency and the right environment matter so much.

We work with stroke and paralysis, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, neuropathy, Bell's palsy and balance and gait disorders, always alongside the patient's neurologist and medical team. You can read more about our full [neurological physiotherapy service](/service/neurological-physiotherapy) and how it is delivered across Bangalore.

## Why home care is the right setting in HSR Layout

For someone with limited mobility, getting to a clinic is often the single biggest barrier to consistent rehab — and in HSR's traffic, it can be exhausting for both patient and family. Home rehabilitation removes that barrier entirely.

It also has a clinical advantage: retraining someone to walk, transfer and manage stairs is far more effective in their own home, on their own surfaces, than in a clinic. We also guide family members and caregivers so the right support continues between sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should stroke rehabilitation start?

Rehabilitation generally begins as early as the medical team advises — earlier, consistent input tends to support better recovery. We coordinate with the treating neurologist and work to their guidance.

### Can you provide Parkinson's physiotherapy at home?

Yes — home programmes for Parkinson's focus on movement, balance, posture and gait to help maintain mobility and independence, alongside the neurologist's care.

### Do you train family members and caregivers?

Yes. Because neuro rehab depends on consistency, we show caregivers how to support safe movement and exercises between sessions.

### How long will recovery take?

Neurological recovery is gradual and varies from person to person. Your physiotherapist sets realistic goals and reviews progress regularly.
