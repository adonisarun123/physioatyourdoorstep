## Sports physiotherapy — built around your sport

Sports physiotherapy treats the injuries active people actually get: ligament and cartilage tears, tendon overuse, muscle strains and joint sprains. The key difference from general physiotherapy is specificity — the loading a runner's knee needs is nothing like the demands on a lifter's shoulder or a footballer's ankle, and a good programme is built around your activity and your goals.

Whether you train at an apartment gym, run around the lake, play weekend football or lift seriously, we match the rehab to what you actually do — and to the level you want to return to.

## Our treatment approach

Recovery is milestone-based, not calendar-based. For a major injury such as an ACL, rehab moves through clear stages — settling pain and swelling, restoring range of motion, rebuilding strength, then adding proprioception and sport-specific work before a return-to-play check — with reassessment at every stage so you progress on readiness, not guesswork.

- Hands-on manual therapy and soft-tissue work
- Progressive strength and loading programmes
- Proprioception, balance and agility retraining
- Taping and bracing where appropriate
- Movement and technique correction to prevent re-injury
- A staged return-to-sport plan with clear milestones
