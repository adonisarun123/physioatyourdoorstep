## Orthopaedic physiotherapy — and who it helps

Orthopaedic physiotherapy treats problems of the musculoskeletal system: the back, neck, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. In Bellandur, that most often means desk-driven lower-back and cervical pain, knee stiffness, frozen shoulder, sciatica and disc-related pain — the everyday toll of long working hours and long commutes.

Every case starts with a proper hands-on assessment. Your physiotherapist checks your posture, joint range, strength and movement patterns, identifies what is actually driving the pain, and builds a personalised plan to reduce it, restore movement and keep it from coming back. The goal is lasting relief by treating the cause — not a temporary fix for the symptom.

## Our treatment approach

There is no one-size-fits-all routine. After the assessment, your physiotherapist combines the techniques your specific condition needs and progresses them as you improve.

- Pain and inflammation control
- Joint mobilisation to restore movement
- Progressive strengthening and endurance work
- Stretching, flexibility and posture correction
- Ergonomic guidance for your desk and home workstation
- Balance, coordination and gait retraining where needed

The programme advances gradually, guided by your pain levels, healing stage and goals — so you build genuine, lasting strength rather than chasing short-term relief.
