## Sports physiotherapy — built around your sport

Sports physiotherapy treats the injuries active people actually get: ligament and cartilage tears, tendon overuse, muscle strains and joint sprains. What sets it apart from general physiotherapy is specificity — the loading a runner's knee needs is nothing like the demands on a lifter's shoulder or a footballer's ankle, so a good programme is built around your sport and your goals.

Whether you train in your community gym, run the clubhouse loops, play weekend football or lift seriously, we match the rehabilitation to what you actually do — and to the level you want to get back to. ## Our treatment approach

Recovery is milestone-based, not calendar-based. For a major injury such as an ACL, rehabilitation moves through clear stages — settling pain and swelling, restoring range of motion, rebuilding strength, then adding proprioception and sport-specific work before a return-to-play check — with reassessment at each stage so you progress on readiness, not guesswork.

- Hands-on manual therapy and soft-tissue work
- Progressive strength and loading programmes
- Proprioception, balance and agility retraining
- Taping and bracing where appropriate
- Movement and technique correction to prevent re-injury
- A staged return-to-sport plan with clear milestones

### How long does ACL rehabilitation take?

Returning to full sport after an ACL injury or reconstruction usually takes several months and is guided by milestones — range, strength, proprioception and sport-specific testing — rather than a fixed date. Your physiotherapist reassesses at each stage.

### Can you treat a gym or CrossFit injury at home in Haralur?

Yes — gym and weight-training injuries are among the most common we see here. We assess the injury, treat it hands-on, and correct the loading or technique that caused it.

### Do I need a doctor's referral?
