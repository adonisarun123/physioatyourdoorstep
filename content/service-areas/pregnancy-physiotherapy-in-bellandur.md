## Pregnancy physiotherapy — support through every stage

Prenatal and postnatal physiotherapy helps women stay comfortable and strong through pregnancy and recover well afterwards. During pregnancy, it eases the back, hip and pelvic pain that a changing body brings, and teaches safe movement, posture and breathing. After delivery, it focuses on gentle core and pelvic-floor recovery, easing postpartum aches and helping you return to daily activity at a sensible pace.

All care is gentle, individualised and delivered in coordination with your obstetrician or gynaecologist, so it fits safely within your pregnancy or recovery.

Support is matched to your stage. In early and mid pregnancy the focus is comfort, posture and safe activity; in the third trimester it shifts to easing pelvic and lower-back load and preparing the body for delivery; and after birth it turns gently to healing, pelvic-floor recovery and rebuilding core strength at a pace that suits you.
