## Orthopaedic physiotherapy — and who it helps

Orthopaedic physiotherapy treats problems of the musculoskeletal system: the back, neck, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. In JP Nagar, two groups come to us most often — working professionals and students with chronic back and neck pain from long sedentary hours, and older residents managing knee pain, frozen shoulder and arthritis.

Every case starts with a thorough hands-on assessment. Your physiotherapist checks posture, joint range, strength and movement, works out what is actually driving the pain, and builds a personalised plan to reduce it, restore movement and stop it returning. The goal is lasting relief by treating the cause — not a short-lived patch on the symptom.

## Our treatment approach

There is no generic routine here. After the assessment, your physiotherapist combines exactly the techniques your condition needs and progresses them as you improve.

- Pain and inflammation control
- Joint mobilisation to restore movement
- Progressive strengthening and endurance work
- Stretching, flexibility and posture correction
- Ergonomic guidance for your desk and work-from-home setup
- Balance, coordination and gait retraining where needed

The programme advances gradually, guided by your pain levels, healing stage and goals — so you build genuine, lasting strength rather than chasing quick, temporary relief.
