## Our treatment approach

Recovery is milestone-based, not calendar-based. For a major injury such as an ACL, rehab moves through clear stages — settling pain and swelling, restoring range of motion, rebuilding strength, then proprioception and sport-specific work before a return-to-play check — with regular reassessment at each stage.

- Hands-on manual therapy and soft-tissue work
- Progressive strength and loading programmes
- Proprioception, balance and agility retraining
- Taping and bracing where appropriate
- Movement and technique correction to prevent re-injury
- A staged return-to-sport plan with clear milestones
