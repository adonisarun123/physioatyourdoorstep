## Geriatric physiotherapy — safe, gentle, and at home

Geriatric physiotherapy supports older adults in staying mobile, steady and independent for as long as possible. It manages the aches and stiffness of ageing joints, rebuilds strength and confidence after an illness or a fall, and — importantly — reduces the risk of future falls, which are one of the biggest threats to a senior's independence.

Care is always gentle and paced to the individual, taking their medical history and stamina into account so that every session is safe and comfortable.

Confidence matters as much as strength. Many seniors quietly cut back on moving around after a fall or a scare, which leads to weakness and, over time, an even higher risk of falling — a downward spiral. Encouraging, achievable physiotherapy breaks that cycle, helping older adults trust their body again and stay active and independent at home.

## Geriatric physiotherapy in Bellandur: why home care matters

Taking a frail or unsteady elder through ORR traffic to a clinic is hard, and sometimes genuinely unsafe. Home care removes that entirely — and it gives the physiotherapist something a clinic never can: a clear view of the real risks around the house. Loose rugs, a slippery bathroom, poor lighting on the stairs, a bed that is too low — these get spotted and addressed directly.

Familiar surroundings also help seniors feel at ease and engage more willingly, which makes each session more effective and less tiring.
