Follow-up sessions run to the plan. Each visit your physiotherapist guides you through exercises, performs hands-on techniques such as joint mobilisation, and applies any necessary modalities. Sessions typically last **45–60 minutes**. We track progress continuously — pain, mobility, strength and function — and progress the exercises as you improve, making sure your technique stays correct. Between visits you'll have simple home exercises, and we show family members how to help.

Throughout the course of treatment we update the plan based on results, and you'll see the improvements measured — increased range of motion, reduced pain scores. If something isn't working or a new issue appears, we adjust. Once you reach your goals we hand over a home exercise programme to maintain your gains and prevent relapse.
