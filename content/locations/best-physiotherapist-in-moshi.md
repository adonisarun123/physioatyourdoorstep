## Why choose home physiotherapy in Moshi

### No commute, so sessions actually happen

Recovery depends on consistency. When therapy comes to you, sessions are not missed because of highway traffic, rain or a cab that did not turn up — and every session builds on the last.

### One-on-one attention, every session

There is no crowded clinic and no distractions. Your therapist watches every repetition, corrects form immediately and adjusts the plan to how you respond. Family members are welcome to join and learn how to help.

Exercises are built around your own stairs, chairs, bed and walls — the environment you need to move confidently in. If your back pain comes from driving or desk work, we look at your actual set-up and fix the posture behind it.

## Our physiotherapy services in Moshi

Every service below is delivered at home, by a physiotherapist matched to your condition.

### [Post-surgical rehabilitation](/service/post-surgical-physiotherapy)

Structured rehab after knee and hip replacements, ACL and ligament repair, spine or shoulder surgery and fracture fixation. Starting early (with your surgeon's approval) reduces stiffness and swelling; we progress steadily to restore joint movement and rebuild muscle — even if you were discharged only days ago.

### [Orthopaedic physiotherapy](/service/orthopaedic-physiotherapy)

Treatment for low back pain, neck pain, sciatica, knee arthritis, frozen shoulder, sprains and strains. We use exercise, stretching, manual therapy and pain-relief modalities (heat, ice, TENS), plus posture and ergonomic advice so the pain does not keep returning.

### [Neurological physiotherapy](/service/neurological-physiotherapy)

Home rehabilitation for stroke, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, cerebral palsy, Bell's palsy and neuropathy. After a stroke, for example, we might practise supported walking in your hallway and arm and hand tasks at your dining table — skills that carry straight into daily life.

### [Sports injury physiotherapy](/service/sports-physiotherapy)

For weekend athletes and gym-goers: ankle sprains, ACL tears, runner's knee, tennis elbow, shoulder strains and muscle tears. We settle pain and swelling first, then rebuild strength and flexibility for your sport — in your living room, society gym or a nearby ground.

### [Geriatric physiotherapy](/service/geriatric-physiotherapy)

Gentle, functional care for arthritis, osteoporosis, post-hospital weakness, balance problems and fall prevention — practising safe sit-to-stand, walking and balance work at your pace, with rails and chairs for support.

### [Pediatric physiotherapy](/service/pediatric-physiotherapy)

Play-based therapy for cerebral palsy, delayed walking and other developmental delays, infant torticollis and teen sports injuries. Children participate better in familiar surroundings, and parents learn the exercises to continue between visits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you provide home physiotherapy in Moshi?

Yes. Our physiotherapists visit homes and housing societies across Moshi and neighbouring Chikhali, Bhosari and Dighi, bringing all the equipment a session needs. There is no clinic trip and no time lost on the highway.

### Is home physiotherapy as effective as clinic-based therapy?

Yes, for the vast majority of conditions. You receive the same evidence-based treatment — therapeutic exercise, manual techniques and portable modalities — at home. Many patients progress faster because they miss fewer sessions and avoid travel fatigue.

### What conditions can be treated with physiotherapy at home in Moshi?

Almost anything you would see a clinic physiotherapist for: back and neck pain, sciatica, joint stiffness, arthritis and post-fracture rehab; sports injuries; stroke, Parkinson's, Bell's palsy and neuropathy; recovery after knee or hip replacement; and paediatric and geriatric conditions.

### How much does home physiotherapy cost in Moshi?

Typically ₹799–₹1,099 per 45–60 minute home session, depending on the condition and the specialist required. Weekly and monthly packages bring the per-session cost down. You get the exact price when you book — no hidden charges.

### How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?

It depends on the condition, its severity, your age and overall health. Many patients feel a difference within a few sessions; a typical plan starts with 8–10 visits before a reassessment. Minor injuries may settle in a few weeks, while post-surgical or neurological rehab can take a couple of months.

### Is physiotherapy at home safe for seniors and post-surgery patients?

Yes. Physiotherapy is low-risk, and home treatment removes the travel risks. Our therapists start gently, use chairs or walls for balance, make sure the space is hazard-free, and follow your doctor's guidance.

### How should I prepare for my first home session?

Clear a quiet space with room to move, wear loose clothing, and keep water, a towel and any medical reports or X-rays handy. Tell us if you use a walker, cane or brace. The therapist brings the equipment.
