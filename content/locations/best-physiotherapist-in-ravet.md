## Why choose home physiotherapy in Ravet

### No commute, so sessions actually happen

Recovery depends on consistency. When therapy comes to you — morning, evening or weekend — sessions are not cancelled because of expressway traffic or the hassle of booking a cab after surgery, and every session builds on the last.

### One-on-one attention, every session

There are no other patients and no distractions. Your therapist adjusts the programme in real time based on how you respond, which usually means faster progress than a generic clinic routine.

### Treatment that fits your actual home

If you have knee arthritis, we train on your actual stairs and sofa. If you work from home with neck strain, we correct posture at your own workstation. Exercises built around your home are the ones you keep doing.

### Safer for seniors and post-operative patients

Home therapy avoids the fall risk of travel and exposure to infections in busy waiting rooms. Our therapists sanitise hands and equipment, bring what they need, and family can join sessions to learn how to support safe movement.

### [Post-surgical rehabilitation](/service/post-surgical-physiotherapy)

Structured rehab after knee or hip replacement, ACL repair, shoulder surgery, spine procedures and fracture fixation. With your surgeon's approval we start early to reduce swelling, restore joint movement, rebuild strength and get you walking safely — even straight after hospital discharge.

### [Orthopaedic physiotherapy](/service/orthopaedic-physiotherapy)

Treatment for back pain, neck pain, knee osteoarthritis, frozen shoulder, tennis elbow, sprains, strains and post-fracture stiffness, using therapeutic exercise, joint mobilisation, stretching and pain-relief techniques (heat, cold, TENS), plus ergonomic advice using your own furniture.

### [Neurological physiotherapy](/service/neurological-physiotherapy)

Home rehabilitation for stroke, Parkinson's disease, spinal cord injury, multiple sclerosis, cerebral palsy and neuropathy, focused on balance, walking, coordination, hand function and independence — practised in the home where those skills have to work.

### [Sports injury physiotherapy](/service/sports-physiotherapy)

Ankle sprains, ACL tears, rotator cuff injuries, runner's knee and tennis elbow, managed through a structured plan: pain and swelling first, then sport-specific strength, flexibility and movement correction, so you return with a lower risk of re-injury.

### [Geriatric physiotherapy](/service/geriatric-physiotherapy)

Gentle, functional care for arthritis, joint stiffness, osteoporosis, balance problems, fall prevention and general weakness — sit-to-stand practice, supported walking and balance drills at your own pace.

### [Pediatric physiotherapy](/service/pediatric-physiotherapy)

Play-based therapy for developmental delays, cerebral palsy, infant torticollis and rehab after a fracture or surgery. Children engage better at home, and parents learn exercises to continue between visits.
