Treatment for back pain, neck pain, sciatica, disc problems, knee pain, frozen shoulder, arthritis and joint stiffness — common in both desk professionals and seniors. We combine manual therapy, targeted exercise, traction and pain-relief modalities, plus ergonomic advice so the problem does not keep returning.

### [Neurological physiotherapy](/service/neurological-physiotherapy)

Home rehabilitation for stroke, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, spinal cord injury, cerebral palsy and peripheral neuropathy. Sessions focus on balance, gait, coordination and independence in daily tasks — practised in the rooms you actually live in, with family trained to reinforce the work between visits.

### [Sports injury physiotherapy](/service/sports-physiotherapy)

For runners, gym-goers, cyclists and racquet players: ankle sprains, ACL and ligament tears, muscle strains, tendinopathy (tennis and golfer's elbow), runner's knee and shin splints. We settle pain first, then rebuild strength and movement patterns specific to your sport, and correct the imbalances behind repeat injuries.

### [Geriatric physiotherapy](/service/geriatric-physiotherapy)

Gentle, age-appropriate care for osteoarthritis, low back pain, osteoporosis, balance problems and fall prevention. We work on sit-to-stand, walking, stair climbing and strength so seniors can stay independent and active at home.

### [Pediatric physiotherapy](/service/pediatric-physiotherapy)

Play-based therapy for developmental delays, cerebral palsy, torticollis in infants and post-injury rehab in teenagers. Children relax and participate better at home, and parents learn the exercises to continue between visits.
