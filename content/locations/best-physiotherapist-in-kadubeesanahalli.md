## How It Works

### 1. Book a Free Consultation

Contact us by phone or through our website to schedule your first session in Kadubeesanahalli. Our coordinator will ask about your condition, your address and your preferred time. We aim to reply quickly, often the same day. Home visits can be booked 7 days a week, with morning and evening slots for working professionals.

### 2. Initial Home Assessment

Our therapist arrives at your home at the agreed time, equipped for a full evaluation. In this first session (about 60 minutes) they discuss your symptoms and medical history and check range of motion, strength, balance and posture. This one-on-one evaluation identifies the root cause of the problem. The therapist explains your condition clearly and may begin light therapy such as manual mobilisation if appropriate.

### 3. Personalised Treatment Plan

Based on the assessment, the therapist designs a customised programme. Back pain might call for core strengthening and traction; post-operative knee rehab for progressive weight-bearing work and mobilisations. The plan sets out how many sessions per week are recommended (usually 2–3) and roughly how many will be needed in total, and it is built around your schedule and home environment. No two patients get the same programme.

### 4. Ongoing Therapy Sessions

Therapy runs to your plan, usually 45–60 minutes per session. Each visit the physiotherapist guides you through exercises, performs hands-on techniques such as soft tissue work or joint mobilisation, and adjusts the programme as you improve. We monitor pain levels, flexibility and strength continuously, adding new exercises when you're ready. You also get home exercises to do between visits, which accelerates healing.

### 5. Progress Tracking

After several sessions we re-evaluate progress against the initial assessment, checking improvements in mobility, pain and functional tasks such as walking or climbing stairs. The plan is refined to focus on what's left. Your therapist will also advise on ergonomics for your home or work setup and any lifestyle adjustments that support recovery.

### 6. Recovery & Maintenance

Once you reach your goals we prepare for discharge, with a final set of home exercises and instructions so you can maintain your gains. If you'd like, we can schedule periodic follow-ups to make sure you stay pain-free.
