## Why choose home physiotherapy in Haralur

### No commute, so sessions actually happen

The single biggest reason rehabilitation programmes fail is that people stop turning up. When the physiotherapist comes to you, that problem largely disappears — and consistency is what drives recovery in almost every condition we treat.

### One-on-one attention, every session

There is no split focus and no waiting room. Your physiotherapist spends the full 45–60 minutes on your assessment, your hands-on treatment and your exercises, correcting form in real time.

### Treatment that fits your actual life

A clinic never sees your workstation, your mattress, your stairs or your bathroom. At home, your physiotherapist can spot the real triggers and build the rehabilitation around the space you actually move through every day.

### Safer for seniors and post-operative patients

Avoiding a car journey in the tender weeks after surgery, or with a frail parent who is unsteady on their feet, removes genuine risk — as well as exposure to a crowded clinic.
